FAQ

Good to know

Where exactly is Al Hawiyah Camp?

On Bidiyah Camps Road near Wahiba Sands in the Bidiyah / Bidiya area of Sharqiyah. Message us for a pin and arrival tips for your vehicle type.

What kind of accommodation is it?

Standalone desert chalets / lodges set on the sand, with traditional crenelated rooftops and warm exterior lighting. Confirm room type and inclusions for your dates on WhatsApp.

Can you help with dune trips?

Yes — ask when you enquire. We can advise on timing for sunrise or sunset sand and how guests usually organise a short Wahiba experience from the camp.

How do I check rates and availability?

Message or call us with your dates. We prefer to quote on WhatsApp so we can match room type, season, and group size accurately.

Is it suitable for families or small groups?

Travellers stay as couples and small groups. Tell us ages and room needs when you enquire and we will advise what works best.

Ready for Wahiba quiet?

Enquire about your dates — we will reply with availability and everything you need to plan the stay.
